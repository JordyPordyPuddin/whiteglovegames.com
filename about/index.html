About

White Glove Games is an independent studio founded by Jordan Fritch. Every game wears the white gloves: small worlds, big table energy, and play that brings friends and family together.

White Glove Games makes cozy, clever games you play around the table with the people you love.

Our games: Badger Mountain (coming soon), HedgeRow (flagship) and Merely Beasts (in development).
