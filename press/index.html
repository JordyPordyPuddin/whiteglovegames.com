Description

Your gnome is the fighter and you’re the trainer in the corner. Take them on a roguelite adventure through the swamps near the stadium, or straight into a fast-paced match in front of the fans.

Play against savvy Gnumb opponents, or host a match for your Steam friends. Gather around the table from anywhere. Spatial voice puts each trainer at their gnome’s corner of the table, so you always know who’s plotting what.

About the studio

White Glove Games is an independent studio founded by Jordan Fritch. Every game wears the white gloves: small worlds, big table energy, and play that brings friends and family together.
