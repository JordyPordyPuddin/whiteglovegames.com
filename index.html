The studio

We’re a small independent studio building games the way you’d build a model village: slowly, by hand, with care in every corner. Our games are meant to be gathered around, whether that’s a real mountain from our hometown, a hedge-lined arena, or a household at the edge of the wild. Pick a world and have a look around.
