Joiner Games

Game night, together, wherever you live.

Joiner is a free social board-game platform set in a mansion on a sea cliff. Your friends show up as pairs of white-gloved hands, wander between the tables, and hear each other through proximity voice, so the people at your table sound close and laughter drifts in from across the hall.

Classic games wait at the tables, and some wings of the house stay locked… for now.
